## Findings

- The existing 11 checks pass. Their scope is numerical; no claims of physical
  grasp success should be drawn from the three-cube attachment replay.
- The calibrated mesh envelope correctly identifies clearance at the old 0.19 rad
  angle; no production grasp setting has been substituted without validation.
- The offline top-down expert was previously only a plan. This branch now provides
  an isolated dynamic implementation, configurable by grasp-depth fraction.
- The first attempted direct mocap-parent implementation did not impart the needed
  physical velocities for frictional lift. It was replaced by a dynamic gripper
  following a weld target; the free cube is not attached to it.
- Whole-finger hulls overlap near the mechanism and approximate concave surfaces.
  Finger-to-finger collision is excluded explicitly. A box-pad mode is a diagnostic
  control, not evidence of robot fidelity.
- Mesh-hull lift/hold succeeds under the current assumptions, but release placement
  misses the 3 mm threshold. This remains a failed full episode in output metrics.
- Hardware, full-arm contact/collision checking, vision, structure compilation,
  trained ML, and a deployment-quality expert are still outstanding.
- The current experiment uses its own table frame and perfect cube XY. Future
  robot-frame and learned-policy contracts must not silently reuse those semantics.
